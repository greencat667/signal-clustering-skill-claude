# Scoring rubric

Score each pattern 1–5 on each dimension, and give a phrase of reasoning for every score. Keep the four scores separate; they answer different questions.

## Momentum: is evidence arriving faster?

Split the time window in half (or compare with the last run, in update mode).

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 1 | Signals are old or stopped arriving; the latest are counter-signals |
| 2 | A trickle, evenly spread or slowing |
| 3 | Steady: similar numbers in both halves of the window |
| 4 | Clearly more signals in the later half, or a recent strong signal |
| 5 | Accelerating: several strong signals recently, from different actors |

Don't mistake reporting volume for momentum. Ten articles about one announcement are one signal (see deduplication in Step 1).

## Spread: is it showing up in different places?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 1 | One actor, one place |
| 2 | A few actors of the same type (e.g. only charities, only one country) |
| 3 | Two types of actor, or two or three places |
| 4 | Three or more types of actor (e.g. government, business and community) or several countries |
| 5 | Broad convergence: different actors in different places, apparently acting independently |

High spread is the best single sign that a pattern is real rather than an artefact of who you follow.

## Novelty: how far is this from what's already assumed?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 1 | Widely known and already in the team's plans |
| 2 | Known, but not yet reflected in plans |
| 3 | Discussed by specialists; not yet mainstream |
| 4 | Surprising to the team; cuts against a current assumption |
| 5 | Surprising even to specialists; few people are talking about it |

A novelty score above 3 should name the assumption or consensus view it departs from.

## Evidence: how solid are the signals?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 1 | Commentary, predictions and press releases only |
| 2 | Mostly announcements and intentions; little data |
| 3 | A mix: some decisions, launches or data alongside commentary |
| 4 | Mostly primary: legislation, budgets, published results, official statistics |
| 5 | Multiple independent primary sources that agree, including hard data |

Lower the score by one if credible counter-signals exist and the pattern's case doesn't answer them.

## Reading the scores together

| Pattern profile | What it usually means | Typical recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| High momentum, high evidence | Something is happening; the question is what to do about it | **Act** |
| High novelty, low evidence | A possible early signal of something important, or a fad | **Research** |
| High spread, low momentum | A slow structural shift | **Act** if relevant, otherwise **Watch** |
| High momentum, low spread | A burst in one sector or place; could spread or fizzle | **Watch**, with a query for the next place it would appear |
| Low on everything | Probably not a pattern | Demote to an outlier |
